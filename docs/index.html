Ambient Mode

When the music deserves the whole screen.

Right-click the track title and turn the display under your pointer into a focused playback scene. This is not a separate fake visualizer. It is the same player state, just given room to breathe.

Vinyl, Cassette, or GlassChange the atmosphere without changing your queue.

New-track HUD

Notice the new song. Not a new interruption.

A lightweight, non-activating panel tells you what just started without stealing keyboard focus or dragging you into the player. Put it at a useful edge, on the display you are using.

+

Placement that is actually yoursChoose from eight useful screen-edge positions in Settings.

+

Motion makes spatial senseEach card enters and exits through its nearest edge. No diagonal nonsense.

+

Long titles remain readableMinimum width, expansion, wrapping, and enough transparent room for the glow.
